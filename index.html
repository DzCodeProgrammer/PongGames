ℹ️ About

Advanced Pong Game

A modern implementation of the classic Pong arcade game with advanced features, AI opponent, and beautiful UI.

Features

  • 🎮 4 Difficulty Levels with Adaptive AI
  • 🎵 Sound Effects & Audio Feedback
  • ✨ Particle Effects & Trail Animation
  • 📊 Advanced Statistics Tracking
  • ⚙️ Fully Customizable Settings
  • 🏆 Best-of-5 Tournament Mode
  • 🎨 Modern UI with Dark Theme
  • 📱 Responsive Design

How to Play

Controls:

  • Move your paddle with the mouse or arrow keys
  • Press SPACE to pause/resume the game
  • First to 5 points wins a round
  • Win the best-of-5 to win the match!
